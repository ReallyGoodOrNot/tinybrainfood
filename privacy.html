Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

"tiny brain food uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the tiny brain food channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok.

What it accesses

Only the permissions needed to publish videos and their captions to the channel's own accounts, and to read back the link of each published post. On TikTok this means the basic profile info and the permission to upload or publish videos.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens, kept privately on the owner's own computer, so daily posting can run automatically. It does not collect, store or process data about any other person, viewer or follower.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared. Data is only sent to the platform being posted to (Google/YouTube, Meta, TikTok), as needed to publish the video.

Other users

The tool is not available to the public and never accesses anyone else's account.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's account settings (for example Google, Facebook/Instagram "Business integrations", Threads "Website permissions", TikTok "Manage app permissions"). Revoking deletes nothing on our side because nothing else is kept.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The platforms' own terms and privacy policies also apply.
